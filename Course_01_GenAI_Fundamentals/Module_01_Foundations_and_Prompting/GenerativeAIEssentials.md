# Intro to Generative AI
AI: simulation of human intelligence by machines.
AI models:
* Learn from massive existing data through training.
* 2 ways to approach that:
** Discriminative AI.
** Generative AI.

# Discriminative AI
* Distinguishes between different classes of data.
* Each data point labeled with its class.
* Use advanced algo. to: Differentiate, Classify, Identify patterns, Draw conclusions.
* Example: Email spam filters.
* Limitations: Cannot understand context, Cannot generate new context.

# Generative AI
* Create new content based on training data.
* Starts as a prompt (Text, Image, video, Other forms of input) to GenAI model and outputs as Text, Images, Audio, Video, Code and Data.
* GenAI develop creativity using those deep learning models (GANs (Generative adversarial networks), VAEs (Variational autoencoders), Transformers and Diffusion models). They can be considered as the building block of GenAI.

# Evolution of GenAI
* 1950s: Origin of ML & used Exploring algorithmic data creation
* 1990s: Neural Networks advanced GenAI.
* 2010s: Deep learning, large datasets and enhanced computing accelerated genAI.
* 2014+: GANs and other models revolutionized generative AI.

# Foundation models:
* AI models with broad capabilities
* Adapted to build specialized and advanced models or tools
* Example: LLMs (Large language models) are trained to process and generate text:
** OpenAI: GPT n-series (GPT 1, GPT 2, GPT-3/3.5 and GPT-4)
** Google: PaLM
** Meta: Llama 
* Example for image generation:
** Stable Diffusion
** DALL-E

Such variety of models led to a growing market of AI tools like:
* Text generation: ChatGPT & Gemini
* Image generation: Dall-E 2 & Midjourney
* Video generation: Synthesia
* Code generation: Copilot & AlphaCode
